GAME MODE

AI DIFFICULTY
Hard reads every hand. All three are first-pass heuristics, to be tuned by playtesting.
SET TYPES
Each option allows its regular and bomb sets. A full house is 2 or more groups of different ranks whose sizes step down by one (3+2, 4+3, 4+3+2, 5+4 ...). Singles are always allowed, and a Garuda can always make an aggregate.

TEAM ASSIGNMENT
All Solo: every player is their own team. Everyone's Arrow is pooled together across the whole table, and the full pool is shared in full by every player who goes out that hand — if you don't go out, you get none of it.
HAND CARD ORDER
Frozen: hands stay in dealt order and you can only play cards that sit next to each other. 1 Time Reorder (trial): automatically sorts one human player's starting hand to group bombs and other strong sets together, using this game's own set-type options; only applies with exactly one human player at the table. The hand is then frozen. Free Order: every hand is kept sorted by rank (suits Hearts, Swords, Diamonds, Coins, Moon, Sun; jokers at the right end) and you can select any cards in your hand, not only adjacent ones. Applies to all seats, including AI.
VP SCORING
BZF: card VP scores Star, Arrow and Radioactive. TP: card VP scores Tokoh Points only. BZF + TP: Star, Arrow, Radioactive and TP all score. None: no card VP at all; instead each player scores +100 / +75 / +50 for going out 1st / 2nd / 3rd in a hand, and the owner of the top set banks 5 VP for every card still on their tableau when a trick is scored. No nothing-on-table penalty in this mode. Team and solo bonuses and the max-size bomb bonus apply in every mode.
STRAIGHTS
Low (default): among straights, running pairs and triples, and their flush bombs, the lower starting rank wins (2–5 beats 3–6). High: the higher starting rank wins (3–6 beats 2–5). Full houses and groups are unchanged.
MAJOR ARCANA (NEW)
On: before each hand the table shows 2 Major cards per player and the lowest-VP player picks first. Your pick sits in your Arcana space (it stays across hands) and you may play one per trick, on your own turn, for an immediate bonus: a suit card pays +5★ per card of that suit on all tableaus, The World +5★ per different suit, The Star doubles all Star VP until the trick ends. Jokers count as any one suit. Used cards are discarded and recycled. Requires VP Scoring BZF or BZF + TP.
