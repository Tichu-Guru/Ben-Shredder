TEAM ASSIGNMENT
All Solo: every player is their own team. Everyone's Arrow is pooled together across the whole table, and the full pool is shared in full by every player who goes out that hand — if you don't go out, you get none of it.
MAJOR ARCANA
Disabled in this version. The Majors are specified in a later pass.
SORTED INITIAL HAND (TRIAL)
Trial feature: automatically sorts one human player's starting hand to group bombs and other strong sets together, using this game's own set-type options. Only applies with exactly one human player at the table; has no effect otherwise. Your hand is still fixed once sorted — you can only play cards that sit next to each other, as usual.
PICK UPS (TRIAL)
Trial feature: on your turn, instead of playing or passing, you may Pick Up 1-3 cards from a single meld belonging to another player (never the current top set, and never a meld with 4 or more cards). Picking up counts as passing — once you pass or pick up, you're done for the rest of that trick except for playing a bomb of your own if you have one. Capped at 1 pick-up per player per trick. A wish that forces you to play still overrides this.
